# Share Any Ayah in Seconds

Assalamu Alaikum,

Reading Quran should be simple, interactive, and easy to share with others.

## How to share a specific ayah

Use this link format:

`/quran/{sura}:{ayah}`

Example (Ayat al-Kursi):

`/quran/2:255`

When someone opens that URL, it goes to the right surah and jumps to that ayah.

## While reading, you can interact in one place

- Play recitation
- Restart from an ayah
- Bookmark an ayah
- Open quick access ayat
- Share directly from bookmark entries

## Recitation flow

Before user-initiated recitation starts, a Bismillah intro clip is played, then the ayah stream continues.

## Why this helps

- Less friction while reading
- Faster sharing of exact verses
- Better collaboration and study circles

If you are teaching, studying, or sending reminders, just share the ayah link format and you are done.

Jazakum Allahu khayran.
